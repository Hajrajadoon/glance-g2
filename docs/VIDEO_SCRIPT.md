# Demo video script (target 2:45, hard limit 3:00)

## Set up before recording
1. Stop the server, then run `npm run server`, `npm run dev` and `npm run sim`.
2. At the glasses' "Link Gmail" screen, press **Click** to use the **demo inbox** (Sara, Ali, Maria, Dad). This keeps real emails and people off the video. Nothing is really sent in demo mode.
3. Close the `.env` tab and anything with keys. Put the Glasses Display window and the Browser window side by side.
4. Optional: show the typed command box for "say no politely" in case your microphone is noisy.
5. Record with Win + Alt + R (Xbox Game Bar), or OBS. Speak slowly. Do a practice run first.

## Script
| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Glasses Display, card 1 | "Email pulls us out of the moment. You are walking to a meeting, and you either ignore your inbox or take out your phone." |
| 0:12 | Same | "This is Glance, a Track 4 app for Even G2. It clears your inbox in thirty seconds, on your glasses." |
| 0:22 | Card 1: Sara. Click Down, Up | "Glance reads my unread Gmail and keeps only the emails that need me. Newsletters, promotions and alerts never appear. Here, one email at a time: who it is from and what they want." |
| 0:50 | Click on Sara's card (draft) | "The reply is already written by AI from the email itself. I do not dictate anything." |
| 1:05 | Type or say "shorter" | "Too long? I say 'shorter'." |
| 1:20 | Draft changes. Say "say no politely" | "Or I can decline. 'Say no politely.' It never invents excuses or names: if the AI adds a detail that is not in the email, Glance throws it away." |
| 1:45 | Click, confirm screen, Click, "Sent" | "When I am happy, one tap to confirm, one tap to send. Nothing is ever sent without that last tap, and the glasses cannot choose who it goes to." |
| 2:05 | Next card, then Double Click | "Before a meeting, a double tap shows a three-line brief: who is in it, the last thread with them and a question to ask." |
| 2:25 | Back to a card | "Under the hood: a TypeScript app on the Even Hub SDK, a Node backend, the Gmail API with pairing-code sign-in, and Whisper for voice. Keys stay on the server and emails are never stored." |
| 2:40 | Glasses showing "Inbox zero" | "Glance: inbox zero, eyes up. Thank you." |

## Tips
- If a step fails on camera, cut and redo that part. You can join clips in Clipchamp.
- Keep each action slow enough to read: about 2 seconds per screen.
- Export at 1080p, check the length, and watch it once with sound before uploading.
